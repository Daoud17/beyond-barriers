Community Development Services

Beyond Barriers provides community-based services that support persons with disabilities and their families, strengthen inclusion, increase awareness, and help create more accessible opportunities within society.

Services

Women Empowerment and Protection

Beyond Barriers provides safe, confidential, and inclusive support for women and girls with disabilities, with a focus on dignity, protection, empowerment, and access to appropriate services.

Our approach aims to strengthen awareness, encourage participation, support individual needs, and help connect women and girls with relevant services and resources when needed.

Combating Gender-Based Discrimination Lebanon

Beyond Barriers provides safe, confidential, and inclusive support for women and girls with disabilities, including guidance, referrals, case support, and assistance in accessing relevant services. Our aim is to promote empowerment, protection, and independence while helping reduce the risks of discrimination, violence, abuse, and social exclusion.

We are committed to respecting the privacy and dignity of every person who reaches out to us. Information shared with Beyond Barriers is handled with care and confidentiality, and each situation is approached according to the individual needs and circumstances of the person seeking support.

If you or someone you know needs assistance, wishes to report a concern, or would like to request guidance, you can contact Beyond Barriers by phone at +961 3 685 440 or by email at info@beyondbarriers-lb.org .

Accessibility Services

Architectural Consultation

Beyond Barriers provides guidance and consultation to help improve accessibility within buildings, facilities, public spaces, educational institutions, workplaces, tourism establishments, and community environments.

Our approach focuses on identifying physical barriers and recommending practical adjustments that support safer, easier, and more independent access for persons with disabilities.

Through accessibility-focused consultation, we aim to encourage institutions and organizations to adopt more inclusive design practices and create environments that can be used comfortably and independently by the widest possible range of people.

Support Services

In-kind and Financial Support

Beyond Barriers provides in-kind and financial support to help persons with disabilities and their families respond to essential needs and difficult living circumstances.

Support may include assistance related to daily living needs, accessibility requirements, rehabilitation, transportation, education, healthcare, emergency situations, or other forms of support depending on the individual situation and available resources.

Each request is considered with respect for dignity, privacy, fairness, and the specific circumstances of the person or family seeking assistance.
