About Us

Beyond Barriers

Beyond Barriers is a Lebanese organization dedicated to advancing the inclusion, participation, and rights of persons with disabilities. We work toward creating a more accessible and inclusive society where every individual has equal opportunities to participate, contribute, and live with dignity.

Through our work, we aim to challenge barriers, promote awareness, encourage participation, and support initiatives that strengthen the inclusion of persons with disabilities within their communities.

Vision

Beyond Barriers envisions an inclusive society where persons with disabilities enjoy equal rights, equal opportunities, and full participation in all aspects of life.

We believe in a society built on dignity, respect, accessibility, social justice, and inclusion — a society where every individual can develop their abilities, make their own choices, and actively contribute to their community.

Mission

Beyond Barriers works to promote and protect the rights of persons with disabilities and to support their access to equal opportunities, meaningful participation, and an improved quality of life. We seek to contribute to a society in which the dignity, independence, choices, and basic rights of persons with disabilities are respected and guaranteed.

Through our programs, services, partnerships, and community-based initiatives, we work to strengthen a culture of inclusion and to promote the right of persons with disabilities to participate fully in social, educational, economic, and community life. We approach disability as an integral human rights and development issue and encourage communities and institutions to move beyond exclusion, dependency, and accessibility barriers.

Our mission is also centered on raising awareness, encouraging positive social change, and working with persons with disabilities, families, communities, institutions, and relevant stakeholders to create environments that are more accessible, equitable, and inclusive.

Advocacy & Rights
